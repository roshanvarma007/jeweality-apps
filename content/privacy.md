This Privacy Policy explains how **JEWEALITY** ("we", "us" or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile applications we publish on Google Play (the "Apps") and this website.

By using our Apps, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Apps.

## Information We Collect

The information an App handles depends on the App and the features you use. The exact data types for each App are listed in the **Data safety** section of that App's Google Play listing.

### Information you provide

Some Apps may let you enter content such as notes, settings or other information you choose to provide. Unless an App states otherwise, this content is stored on your device.

### Information collected automatically

Where an App uses third-party services (see below), those services may automatically collect information such as device information, app usage and diagnostic data, and advertising identifiers, as described in their own privacy policies.

## How We Use Information

Information is used to:

- Provide, maintain and improve the Apps.
- Understand how the Apps are used and fix problems.
- Show advertising in Apps that are supported by ads.
- Respond to your messages and support requests.

## Third-Party Services

Some of our Apps may use third-party services that have their own privacy policies. These may include:

- [Google Play Services](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Google AdMob](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)
- [Google Analytics for Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- [Firebase Crashlytics](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

Please review the privacy policies of these providers to understand how they handle information.

## Advertising

Apps that display ads may use advertising identifiers to show ads and measure their performance. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android device settings.

## Data Retention and Security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy. We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## Children's Privacy

Our Apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

## Your Choices

- You can stop all data handling by an App by uninstalling it.
- You can manage app permissions in your device settings.
- You can contact us to ask about information related to you.

## This Website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and operations.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
